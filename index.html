The weather fills itself in.

Log the hunt and Blind Guide pulls that morning's conditions for you — sky, temperature, the barometric trend, and the wind hour by hour from first light. You record the birds. It records everything else.

The season, read back

The numbers you'd never keep on paper.

Success rate, birds per hunt, shot efficiency, and every species you put on the strap — tallied as you log, season by season. No spreadsheet, no shoebox of notes.

It keeps going past the headline numbers, too: your best blind, your best day of the week, and how you actually do by moon phase, sky, temperature and wind.

Every blind on one map.

Drop a pin on the marsh, the timber, the cut corn. Blind Guide keeps every hunt tied to its spot, so the history is there whenever you need it.

Mark each blind on the water and set the wind it wants. When that wind shows up in the forecast, Blind Guide flags the blind by name.
