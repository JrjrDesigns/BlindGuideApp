Support

Answers to the common questions are below. If yours isn't here, email us — a real person reads every message.

Contact us

support@trailmarkops.com

We reply within two business days. Tell us the email address on your Blind Guide account, and whether you use the iPhone app or the web app — it gets you an answer faster.

Signing in

I forgot my password

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot? next to Password — in the iPhone app, or at app.blindguideapp.com.
  2. Enter the email you signed up with. We'll send a reset link, good for one hour.
  3. Open the link, choose a new password, then sign in with it — on your phone, the web, or both.

Nothing arriving? Check your spam folder, and make sure it's the address you signed up with.

Can I use the same account on my phone and the web?

Yes. Sign in with the same email and password and your hunts, locations and blinds are all there. A Pro subscription follows your account, too — it doesn't matter where you bought it.

Subscriptions & billing

Logging hunts is free, with no limit. Blind Guide Pro is $8.99/month or $49.99/year and adds the full forecast, per-blind wind matching, season analytics and CSV export. How you manage it depends on where you subscribed.

I subscribed in the iPhone app

Your subscription is billed by Apple, so Apple handles changes, cancellations and refunds.

  • To cancel or switch plans: in Blind Guide, go to Profile → Manage account → Manage subscription. Or open your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Blind Guide.
  • Refunds: request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds for App Store purchases.

I subscribed on the website

Your subscription is billed by Stripe, on our behalf.

  • To cancel, update your card or see invoices: sign in at app.blindguideapp.com and go to Profile → Manage account → Billing & payment method.
  • Off-season: annual subscribers can pause instead of cancelling — Profile → Manage account → Pause my subscription.
  • Refunds: see our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Charged in error? Email us.

What happens when I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, and you aren't charged again. Your journal stays yours: every hunt, location and photo remains fully available on the free plan.

New phone, or reinstalled the app?

Sign in with your account. If Pro doesn't show up, go to Profile → Manage account → Restore purchases. You'll never need to pay twice.

In the field

Does Blind Guide work without signal?

Mostly. Anything you've opened while you had signal — your hunts, forecast, stats and locations — is saved on your phone and stays readable in the marsh. The app tells you when you're looking at a saved copy, and when it was saved.

Logging a new hunt needs a connection, because Blind Guide looks up that day's weather when you save it.

Dropping a pin where I'm standing doesn't work

Blind Guide needs permission to use your location for that. On iPhone, open Settings → Blind Guide → Location and choose While Using the App. Location is only used when you ask for it — to place a spot or blind.

Your data

Can I get a copy of my hunts?

Pro members can export their whole hunt history as a spreadsheet (CSV) from Profile → Export Data.

How do I delete my account?

In the app or on the web, go to Profile → Manage account → Delete my account and confirm with your email and password. Your account and everything on it is erased after 30 days; until then you can change your mind from the same screen.

Subscribed through Apple? Deleting your account doesn't stop Apple billing you. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions as well. Website subscriptions are cancelled for you automatically when you delete.

Anything else — a bug, a feature you'd like, a spot where the forecast seems off — email support@trailmarkops.com. For how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
