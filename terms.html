2. The Service

Blind Guide is a field journal and forecast for waterfowl hunters. It comes in two tiers.

Free. The journal is free, with no hunt limit and no expiration, whether or not you ever subscribe. It includes:

  • Unlimited hunt logging, including saved spots, blinds, harvest, notes and photos
  • Automatic weather logging on every hunt
  • Your season totals — birds, days afield and species
  • A two-day forecast for one of your saved spots

Blind Guide Pro. A paid, auto-renewing subscription that adds:

  • A Hunt Score forecast for every spot you hunt
  • Which blind to hunt, matched to the wind
  • Hunt Scores tuned to what has actually produced for you
  • Season analytics by species, spot, blind, weather and moon
  • The full conditions on every hunt
  • CSV export of everything you've logged

We may add, change, or remove features over time. If a change materially reduces what Pro includes, we'll tell you before it takes effect.
