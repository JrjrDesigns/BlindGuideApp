Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This policy covers Blind Guide Pro subscriptions, billed through Stripe by Trailmark Ops, LLC.

Your journal is always free

Your hunt log, saved spots, and photos are yours and remain fully accessible whether you're on the free tier, an active Pro subscription, or a lapsed one. Nothing below affects your access to what you've logged.

No refunds

Subscription payments are non-refundable, including partial periods, unused time, or lapses in use. Instead of a refund, annual subscribers can pause their subscription (see below).

Pause instead of cancel (annual plans)

Annual subscribers can pause their subscription rather than cancel it — useful in the off-season. Pausing stops future billing; your Pro features pause with it, and your journal stays fully accessible. You choose the resume date, and can resume early at any time.

Canceling

  • Monthly plans: cancel anytime from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep Pro access until then, with no further charges after.
  • Annual plans: you can cancel outright, but we'll offer the pause option first. If you cancel, it takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

Billing issues

If you believe you were charged in error (e.g., a duplicate charge or a billing bug), contact us at support@blindguide.com and we'll look into it. This is separate from the no-refund policy above, which applies to standard subscription charges.

This document is a template drafted for Trailmark Ops, LLC and has not been reviewed by an attorney. A strict no-refund policy can run into friction with certain state consumer-protection rules or card-network dispute standards — worth a quick sanity check with a licensed Ohio attorney before you rely on it.
