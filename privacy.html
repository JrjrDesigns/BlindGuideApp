Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Blind Guide is operated by Trailmark Operations LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Trailmark Operations," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Blind Guide iPhone app, the web app at app.blindguideapp.com, and this website (together, the "Service"); how we collect and use it; who we share it with; how long we keep it; and how you can delete it.

The short version: we collect what you need to keep a hunting journal and get a forecast. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored only in hashed form), plus a name and any other profile details you choose to add. We assign your account a user ID.

Your hunt journal. When you log a hunt, we store what you enter: dates and times, the spots and blinds you hunted, species and harvest counts, notes, and any photos you add.

Location. The spots and blinds you save include precise map coordinates. We store the locations you choose to save and use them to attach weather to your hunts and to build your forecast. We don't track your location in the background.

Weather and conditions. When you log a hunt, the Service looks up the weather, wind, barometric pressure and moon phase for that hunt's location and time and saves them with the hunt.

Purchases. Subscriptions bought in the iPhone app are processed by Apple; subscriptions bought on our website are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We receive and keep limited purchase information — your plan, subscription status, renewal or expiration date, and a transaction ID — so we can turn Pro on for your account.

Support messages. If you contact us, we keep your message and our reply.

Technical information. We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Like any online service, our hosting providers process basic technical information — such as your IP address and device or browser type — when your device connects, in order to deliver the Service and protect it from abuse. We don't use it to build a profile of you.

2. How We Collect It

  • From you — when you sign up, log a hunt, save a spot, upload a photo, or contact us.
  • Automatically — weather and conditions looked up for the hunts you log, and technical information sent when your device connects.
  • From payment processors — Apple or Stripe tell us when you buy, renew or cancel a subscription.

3. How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Run your hunt journal, saved spots, and season totals
  • Generate your forecast and Hunt Scores, and (for Pro subscribers) season analytics
  • Process purchases and manage your subscription
  • Send account, billing and service emails, and — only if you opt in — seasonal or newsletter emails
  • Answer support requests
  • Keep the Service secure, fix problems, and improve it
  • Meet legal obligations

We may also use logged hunt data in de-identified, aggregated form — with your name, email and account removed — to improve the forecast model. We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

5. When We Share Information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service providers listed above, to run the Service
  • If required by law, subpoena or other legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we'll notify you and this policy will continue to apply to your information
  • With your consent

6. How Long We Keep It, and How to Delete It

While your account is open, we keep your journal and account information so you can use them. Your journal is never deleted or hidden because a subscription lapses or is downgraded.

To delete your account, go to Profile → Manage account → Delete my account in the app or on the web and confirm. Your account and everything in it — profile, hunts, spots, blinds and photos — is permanently erased after 30 days. Until then, you can cancel the deletion from the same screen. Copies in our backups are removed as those backups expire on their normal schedule. We keep only what the law requires, such as billing records for tax purposes, and de-identified aggregate data that can't be linked back to you.

Subscribed through Apple? Deleting your account doesn't stop Apple from billing you. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions as well. Website subscriptions are canceled automatically when you delete your account.

Can't sign in? Email support@trailmarkops.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correct. You can see and edit your profile and every journal entry in the app.
  • Get a copy. Pro subscribers can export their journal as a CSV in the app. Anyone, on any plan, can email us for a copy of their data.
  • Delete. Delete your account and its data at any time, as described above.
  • Withdraw consent. You can turn off location or photo access for Blind Guide at any time in your iPhone's Settings. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in any of them.

Depending on where you live (for example, California, the EU or the UK), you may have additional rights, such as to object to or restrict certain processing. We honor access, correction and deletion requests from all users regardless of where they live. Email support@trailmarkops.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Blind Guide is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. Users aged 13 to 17 may use Blind Guide only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and access limited to the people and providers who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Where Your Data Is Stored

We're based in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. By using Blind Guide outside the U.S., you understand your information will be transferred there.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you by email or in the app before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
